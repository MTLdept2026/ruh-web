Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Ruh is a personal Islamic reflection and remembrance companion. This policy explains what information the app stores, what leaves your device, and how prayer reminders work.

Information stored on your device

Most app data stays on your device in browser storage. This can include your name or nickname, diary reflections, salawat and dhikr progress, mood selections, preferences, saved location label, and app settings. We do not operate user accounts for this app.

Prayer reminder notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders, the app sends notification subscription details to our notification service so reminders can be delivered. This may include your push subscription endpoint and keys, reminder schedule, timezone, saved location label, notification permission status, whether the app is running as an installed app, browser user agent, browser language, and the app origin.

This information is used only to schedule and send the reminders you enabled. You can turn reminders off in the app, which asks the service to delete the subscription record for your device.

Location

Ruh uses your saved location or city to calculate prayer times and qibla direction. The app is designed around manual city/location setup. If your browser or device asks for location or compass-related permission, that access is used to provide prayer-time or qibla features.

Third-party services

The app may request prayer-time data from public prayer-time services and is hosted on Netlify. Netlify Functions and Netlify Blobs are used for prayer reminder subscription storage and delivery support.

Analytics and advertising

Ruh does not include advertising SDKs and does not use analytics SDKs in this version.

Data deletion

You can delete local app data by clearing the app or browser storage on your device. Turning off prayer reminders in the app deletes the stored push subscription for that device. For help with deletion requests, contact the developer using the contact details shown in the Google Play listing.

Children

Ruh is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact the developer using the Google Play listing contact details.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will be available at this page.

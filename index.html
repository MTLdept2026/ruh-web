How to move through Ruh
01
Begin with the Heart
Tap the word that is most present. Ruh writes it into the page, then turns the verse and the day’s guidance toward that state.
02
Use the Day’s Thread
Save the line if it speaks to you, or begin the suggested wird. The practice is saved quietly into today’s lunar record.
03
Read the Verse Slowly
Tap the verse to contemplate. Swipe it sideways when you want another verse with the feel of turning a page.
04
Mark Prayer Notes
In Diary, tap a prayer name when that prayer mattered that day. It is a mark of remembrance, not a score.
05
Read the Lunar Record
An open circle is an untouched day. A gold circle has reflection. A small dot below means prayer was noted. A soft halo marks today.
06
Return Over Time
As the record gathers, Ruh returns gentle patterns: what themes keep appearing, which prayers became doorways, and what you may want to carry again.
07
Light and Dark
Tap the sun or moon in the top corner to switch between parchment and nightfall. Ruh keeps the chosen mode steady until you change it.
Nothing here is a streak. Blank days remain open.
